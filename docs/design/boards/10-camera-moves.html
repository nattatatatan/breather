Where the camera goes
Transitions
Begin — the camera walks in
No cut. The figure grows to fill the frame over about two seconds while the wordmark, the button and the nav dissolve. Nothing is added; things only leave.
Circle — the camera passes behind
The view swings around the figure and settles behind its head. You stop looking at the object and look out from where it sat — others, small and far, sitting too.
Fire — the world recedes
Ground, stones and logs fade over the length of the sitting until one small flame is left in the dark. The app never refreshes the image to hold attention.
