Mai asks how to practise metta — and gets an answer worth having
User story
01
She sits thirty minutes — samatha, breath, intention kindness — and writes one honest note at the end.
02
She posts a question to the circle and attaches that sitting, so nobody has to guess what she actually did.
03
Ari, six years in, sees the thread in the circle and taps the attached sitting rather than answering from the title.
04
He reads the conditions: mode, intention, object, length, where she returned, and her note. Then her practice history.
05
His reply names what happened in her sitting — the two warm minutes, the mismatch of object and intention — not generic metta instructions.
